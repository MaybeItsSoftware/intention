Welcome to Intention

Intention puts a moment of choice between you and the things that pull you in. Open a blocked site or app and it stops you, then asks what you actually meant to do.

    This takes about a minute. Nothing starts blocking until you finish, and if you stop halfway you'll pick up where you left off.

    Turn on the Safari extension

    Website blocking runs inside Safari, and only you can switch it on, because iOS doesn't let an app do it for you. Apps are handled separately, later in this setup.
