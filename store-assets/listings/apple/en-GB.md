Name: Intention
Subtitle: Browse with a reason
Category: Productivity
Keywords: focus,distractions,screen time,website blocker,habits,intention,AI coach

Intention helps you pause before distracting websites and apps. Choose what you want to protect your time from, set an intention for each target, and say why you want to visit before a pass starts. Your reason stays visible in the floating window while you use the time you chose.

Set a number of visits and minutes for each visit, or set a daily time budget and choose how much of the remaining time to spend now. When your intention is used up, your AI coach can talk through your reason and decide whether a short extra pass makes sense. Changes that give you more time usually wait until tomorrow.

The Today view shows how your time adds up by website and, where the device permits it, by app. Familiar marks make the breakdown easy to scan. On iPhone and iPad, Screen Time provides an aggregate for selected apps rather than individual app names or icons.

You can keep most of Reddit behind the gate while allowing a particular subreddit or post. The coach can also remember what you are working on, through the conversations you choose to have, and help you return to it.

Coaching credit can be purchased in the app. It is spent only when you message the AI coach and does not expire. Your settings, usage history, and reasons stay on your device.
