Name: Intention
Short description: Explain each visit to a distracting site, set daily limits, and see where your time goes.
Category: Productivity

Intention pauses distracting websites before they pull you away from your plans. Set a rule for each site: a number of visits with minutes per visit, or a maximum number of minutes for the day. With a daily budget, you decide how much of the time left to spend on each visit.

A visit starts with a reason. That reason stays visible in the floating window while you browse. When your intention is used up, the AI coach can discuss what you need to do and decide whether a short extra pass fits. Changes that loosen your rules usually wait until tomorrow.

The Today view adds up time on your blocked sites, shows a breakdown by website and recent day, and uses familiar site marks. You can also allow specific subreddits or posts while leaving the rest of Reddit behind the gate.

Your settings, reasons, and usage history are stored locally. AI coaching uses your own API key, configured under AI access in Settings.
